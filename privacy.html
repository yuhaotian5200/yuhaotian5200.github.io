Privacy Policy — MYAI Drive Sync

Effective date: 28 October 2026

MYAI Drive Sync ("the app") is a personal utility used by one person, the owner of the Google Account it is authorised against. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. What Google user data is accessed

  • The app uses Google Drive API access granted by the account owner during setup.
  • The configured transfer lists, creates and updates files in designated folders in the owner's Drive. The requested Drive scope may allow broader access than those folders.
  • The transfer is configured for Google Drive; its source files are selected from local folders.

2. How the data is used

  • Files are copied from the owner's own computer to the owner's own Google Drive, so the same documents are available in both places.
  • Other tools connected by the owner may access the copies under their own settings and policies.

3. Storage and retention

  • Uploaded copies live in the owner's Google Drive and are governed by Google's own terms and retention settings.
  • The app keeps no server and no database of its own. A local log of file-transfer status is kept on the owner's computer.
  • Files remain until the owner deletes them. Deleting a file in Drive does not delete the owner's local original; the app never propagates deletions in either direction.

4. Sharing

  • The transfer sends selected files to the owner's Google Drive. Other tools connected to those folders may access the copies under their own settings and policies.

5. Security

  • Authorisation tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer in a file readable only by the owner's user account.

6. Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access prevents new authorized transfers; existing Drive copies remain subject to the owner's Drive settings.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and has no users other than the account owner.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
